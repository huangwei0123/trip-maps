PhotoOnMaps User Guide

Turn geotagged photos into an interactive map, then save and share your trip.

1. Add photos or a KMZ

Choose photos from your computer or phone, or drag them onto the photo area. Photos need GPS location metadata to appear as map pins.

You can also open an existing .KMZ file or load a KMZ path using the field in the sidebar.

2. Explore your map

  • Use 3D Terrain to toggle terrain, and Connect path to show or hide the route.
  • Choose Satellite, City Streets, Terrain / Topographic, or a light/dark gray background from Map style.
  • Select a photo in the list or a pin to focus that location.
  • Use Demo Hike to try the map. Reset 3D View fits the map to your pins.

3. Download or save a trip

Download .KMZ creates a file you can open in Google Earth. To host and share a trip from PhotoOnMaps, sign in, select Save to Site, name the trip, then save the KMZ or images.

Saving trips requires a confirmed email address. Your saved trips are available from My Trips.

4. Account, plans & help

Use the account menu to sign out. To recover access to your account, choose Forgot password? in the sign-in window. Open Service for Usage, this guide, and your subscription plan.

  • Usage shows login activity, saved links, and storage.
  • Subscription plan shows your storage usage and available plans.
  • For technical account setup, consult the site's deployment documentation.
